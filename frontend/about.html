About AquaSure

Groundwater Quality Analytics Platform — BIS IS 10500:2012

What is AquaSure?

AquaSure is a groundwater quality analytics platform that implements the Weighted Arithmetic Water Quality Index (WAWQI) based on Bureau of Indian Standards IS 10500:2012. It provides district-level analysis of groundwater quality and an individual water assessment tool.

The platform covers two main modules: Module 1 — District-wise groundwater quality analysis using historical data, and Module 2 — Individual water sample assessment with BIS compliance checking, recommendations, and PDF report generation.

WQI Methodology (WAWQI)

The Weighted Arithmetic Water Quality Index (WAWQI) is computed using the following steps:

Step 1 — Sub-Index Calculation (qi)

For pH: qi = (|value − 7.0| / 1.5) × 100

For all other parameters: qi = (value / acceptable limit) × 100

Step 2 — Normalize Weights

wi = raw weight of parameter i
Normalized weight = wi / Σ(all active parameter weights)

Step 3 — Compute WQI

WQI = Σ (normalized_weight_i × qi_i)

Strict Potability Rules

In addition to the WQI score, potability is determined by critical parameter thresholds. Any breach of the following makes water non-potable regardless of grade:

  • Nitrate (NO₃) > 45 mg/L — risk of methemoglobinemia (blue baby syndrome)
  • Fluoride (F) > 1.5 mg/L — risk of fluorosis
  • pH outside 6.5–8.5 — corrosive or scaling water
  • TDS > 2000 mg/L — high dissolved solids affecting taste and health
